Brokerage identity

Riley Hoagland is an Oregon-licensed real estate broker affiliated with Oregon Life Homes, Oregon license #201226143. CoastalRiley.com is Riley’s personal marketing website. It is not a separate brokerage, company or real estate team.

Property information

Property information on this site is provided as a convenient overview and may change. Price, status, measurements, zoning, schools, HOA information, rental eligibility, permit status and other facts should be independently verified. Property photography and listing content are displayed only when Riley has authorization or rights to use them. Where authorized local photography has not yet been added, this site links to the official MLS source instead of republishing third-party syndicated imagery.
